# Launch Post Draft

I made a free client intake questionnaire for freelance developers.

It is designed to catch the project risks that usually appear too late:

- vague scope
- no clear decision owner
- missing access
- unapproved budget
- deadlines without tradeoffs

Free template:

https://github.com/GF-Praticx/gainagent-lab

If you want the polished bundle, I am also preparing a PDF-ready version with a
scope-risk scoring sheet and email templates.
